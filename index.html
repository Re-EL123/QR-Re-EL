About Re-EL QR Studio
Re-EL QR Studio
Version 1.0.0 · Browser-based
A local-first QR code generator and scanner by Re-EL Branding & Technologies.
Privacy by design
QR content is processed in your browser. External library files may load from their CDNs. Scanned destinations are never opened automatically.
Supported capabilities
QR generation, visual customization, logo embedding, PNG/SVG export, image decoding, camera scanning, local project storage, and JSON backup/restore.
